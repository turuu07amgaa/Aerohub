AeroSense Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

1. Location Data

AeroSense accesses your device's approximate or precise GPS coordinates solely when you use the app to fetch real-time local air quality and weather data from Open-Meteo APIs. Your coordinates are never stored on our servers, sold, or shared with advertisers.

2. Local Storage

App preferences (such as your selected language, Air Buddy character, and saved cities) are stored locally on your own device using browser storage.

3. Contact

For any questions regarding this app or privacy policy, contact the AeroSense development team.
